# Placed models you are allowed to stand on.
#
# The server's collision mesh is the terrain the server cares about - the
# ground a player may walk over - and furniture is not in it. A bench stops you
# because its sides are there and its top is not, so you jump the 0.74 units
# over an obj_chair01 and land back on the plaza having passed through it.
#
# Naming a model here adds its drawn geometry to the collision as ground, so
# landing on it, stepping up onto it and falling off it all work the way they
# do anywhere else. Only faces shallow enough to stand on are taken, so a
# chair's legs and the underside of a table are not offered as floor.
#
# One model name per line. Keep it to things a person could plausibly stand on
# in the real game: this is a deliberate list rather than "everything drawn",
# because making all scenery walkable would let you climb the walls.
#
# Southern San d'Oria's plaza, which is where this started - the benches ringing
# the fountain and the crates beside them.
obj_chair01
id_box01
id_box02
